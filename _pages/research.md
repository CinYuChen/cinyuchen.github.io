My research interests include

- Probability Theory
- Stochastic Analysis
- Martingale Theory
- Mathematical Finance

Core Interest & Mathematical Taste｜數學品味與核心關注
   I can enjoy abstract mathematics for its own sake, but what makes probability especially interesting to me is that many abstract concepts gain an additional layer of interpretation. For example, a $\sigma$-algebra can be viewed as information, and conditional expectation can be understood in terms of prediction given that information. I find that extra layer of meaning especially appealing.

Discovery of Probabilistic Elegance｜機率結構的優雅與啟蒙
  The first time I came to appreciate the elegance of martingales was during a lesson built on David Williams’s Probability with Martingales. I was fascinated by the nature of martingale theory—specifically, how a simple probabilistic structure could lead to so many powerful arguments and simplify complex situations. For instance, a martingale converges easily under simple conditions and retains its desirable properties when interacting with stopping times.
  
Bridging Theory and Application｜理論與具體問題的連結

Philosophy of Mathematical Finance｜對數理金融的直覺與思考What attracts me most to mathematical finance is not simply calculating a price, but how finance brings probabilistic ideas to life and how probabilistic results, in turn, provide precise mathematical interpretations for financial concepts. For example, I was thrilled to see how martingales and the no-arbitrage principle connect, both reflecting a sense of fairness in different ways while collaborating to build a theoretical foundation for pricing.While studying, I was naturally curious: “Why can this quantity be called a price?” and “Does it make sense for both the seller and the buyer?” I tried not only to understand the statements and proofs of individual results, but also to reconstruct why each concept was introduced and how these results fit into a coherent pricing framework.
