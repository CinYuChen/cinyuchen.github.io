I am a PhD applicant interested in Probability Theory, Martingale Theory, Stochastic Analysis, and Mathematical Finance.

I obtained my MSc in Applied Mathematics from National Chengchi University, Taiwan, under the supervision of [Prof. Shuenn-Jyi Sheu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=FMiMcUYAAAAJ&hl=en) and Prof. Lung-Chi Chen. My master's thesis focused primarily on discrete-time martingale theorys and their applications to the Kalman filter. Including a conceptual and exploratory study of  the Kalman filter and its role in partial information control problems. ([*A Study of Discrete-Time Martingales with Applications to the Kalman Filter*](/files/THesis.pdf)).

 

My undergraduate years at National Dong Hwa University involved majoring in Applied Mathematics (in division of Statistical Science) and also minoring in Arts & Design. For a glimpse beyond mathematics, please visit the ["Another Side of Me"](https://cinyuchen.github.io/another-side/) page.
